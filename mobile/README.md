# FitDays - Marketplace Listing

## App details:

- **Name**: FitDays
- **Short Description**: Build consistency with personalized workouts and daily reminders.

-- **Full Description**:
FitDays is a modern fitness app designed to help you stay consistent with your workouts and build long-term healthy habits.
Whether you're a beginner or experienced, FitDays creates personalized workout plans based on your goals, fitness level, and schedule.

Stay motivated with daily workout reminders, track your progress, and maintain your streaks. With a clean and intuitive interface, FitDays makes it easy to focus on what matters most — showing up and improving every day.

## Key features include:

Personalized workout plans
Daily workout reminders
Workout tracking and guided sessions
Streak system to build consistency
Clean and minimal user experience
